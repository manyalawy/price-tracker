Common Questions

How do I add a product to track?

Copy the URL of any product page from a supported store, tap the + tab in Dipp, paste the URL, and tap "Start Tracking".

Why am I not receiving notifications?

Make sure notifications are enabled for Dipp in your iPhone's Settings → Notifications. Also check that notifications are turned on inside the Dipp Settings screen.

How often are prices checked?

Dipp checks prices automatically in the background. You'll be notified as soon as a price drops below the original price at the time you started tracking.

How do I delete my account?

Go to the Settings tab inside the app and tap "Delete Account". This permanently removes all your data.
